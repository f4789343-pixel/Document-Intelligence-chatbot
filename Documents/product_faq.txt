PRODUCT FAQ: NORTHSTAR TASKFLOW

What is TaskFlow?
TaskFlow is a project coordination application for small and medium-sized teams. It helps teams organize work, assign owners, track deadlines, and share progress updates.

Who can create a workspace?
Any user with a verified company email address can create one workspace. Additional workspaces require an administrator's approval.

How do I invite a teammate?
Open Workspace Settings, select Members, choose Invite Member, and enter the teammate's work email address. The invitation expires after 7 days.

Can tasks have deadlines?
Yes. A task can have a due date, priority, description, and one assigned owner. A task may also include multiple collaborators.

Does TaskFlow send notifications?
TaskFlow sends in-app notifications for assignments, comments, and approaching deadlines. Email notifications can be configured under Personal Settings.

What happens when a workspace is archived?
Archived workspaces become read-only for regular members. Workspace administrators can restore an archived workspace within 90 days.

Where can I report a bug?
Use Help > Report a Problem inside the application. Include the steps to reproduce the issue and, if possible, a screenshot with confidential information removed.
