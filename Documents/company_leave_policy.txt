COMPANY LEAVE POLICY
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Purpose
This policy explains the leave entitlements and approval process for employees of Northstar Solutions.

2. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave is credited monthly at 1.5 days per month. Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward leave must be used by 31 March.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required when an employee takes more than 2 consecutive working days of sick leave.

4. Applying for leave
Employees should submit annual leave requests through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the intended start date. Managers normally respond within 2 working days. Emergency leave should be reported to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

5. Public holidays
Company-recognized public holidays are separate from annual leave and are listed in the employee calendar.

6. Contact
Questions about this policy should be sent to hr@northstarsolutions.example.
