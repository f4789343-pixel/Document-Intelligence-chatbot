IT SUPPORT HANDBOOK
Northstar Solutions - Internal Guide

1. Support hours
The IT help desk operates Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local office time, excluding company holidays.

2. Creating a ticket
Employees should create a support ticket through the Service Desk portal. Include the device name, a description of the problem, the time it started, and any error message. Do not include passwords or authentication codes.

3. Priority levels
Priority 1: A critical outage affecting most employees or a business-critical service. The target initial response time is 30 minutes during support hours.
Priority 2: A major issue affecting a team or an important workflow. The target initial response time is 4 business hours.
Priority 3: A standard issue affecting one employee with a workaround available. The target initial response time is 1 business day.
Priority 4: A request for information or a routine software installation. The target initial response time is 3 business days.

4. Password problems
Use the approved password-reset page. IT staff will never ask an employee to share their current password. If multi-factor authentication is unavailable, create a ticket and contact the help desk by phone.

5. Laptop security
Install operating-system updates when prompted. Keep company devices locked when unattended. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT immediately.

6. Equipment requests
New monitors, keyboards, and other standard peripherals require manager approval before procurement.
